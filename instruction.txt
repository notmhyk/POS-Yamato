1. RUNNING ON ANDROID TABLET / PHONE (Over Wi-Fi)
==============================================================================

To use your Android tablet as a wireless touch POS terminal:

1. Make sure your PC and your Android tablet are connected to the SAME Wi-Fi network.
2. On your PC, double-click "run_app.bat" (or run "python server.py").
3. Look at the terminal window on the PC. It displays your local network IP, e.g.:
   > On Tablet / Phone (Wi-Fi): http://192.168.1.50:8080
4. Take your Android tablet, open Google Chrome, and type that exact address:
   http://192.168.1.50:8080
5. The Yamato Shokudo POS will instantly load on your tablet with touch-optimized buttons!

==============================================================================
2. INSTALLING AS A FULL-SCREEN STANDALONE APP ON TABLET (WebAPK)
==============================================================================

Once you open the POS in Chrome on your Android Tablet:

1. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner of Chrome.
2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3. Confirm the prompt: "Install Yamato Shokudo POS".
4. The Yamato Shokudo icon will appear on your tablet's home screen.
5. Tapping this icon opens the POS in full-screen mode like a native Android app 
   (without browser address bars or navigation buttons).

3. RUNNING ON WINDOWS PC (Desktop, Laptop, or Touchscreen Terminal)
==============================================================================

OPTION A: Quick 1-Click Launch (Easiest)
----------------------------------------
1. Open this folder on your computer.
2. Double-click "run_app.bat".
3. Your browser will automatically launch the POS at:
   http://localhost:8080

OPTION B: Manual Launch via Terminal
------------------------------------
1. Open PowerShell or Command Prompt in this folder.
2. Type: python server.py
3. Open your browser to: http://localhost:8080

HOW TO INSTALL AS A DESKTOP APP ON PC:
1. Open the POS in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2. Click the "Install" icon in the address bar (or menu ⋮ > "Install Yamato Shokudo POS").
3. The POS will run in its own dedicated, clean window with a desktop shortcut!

4. RUNNING ON APPLE IPAD / IPHONE (iOS)
==============================================================================

1. Connect your iPad to the same Wi-Fi network as the PC running the POS server.
2. Open Safari on your iPad.
3. Type the network IP shown on your PC terminal (e.g., http://192.168.X.X:8080).
4. Tap the "Share" button (square with an up arrow).
5. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" > "Add".
6. The POS icon will be placed on your iPad home screen.

5. CONVERTING TO A STANDALONE ANDROID APK FILE (.apk)
==============================================================================

METHOD 1: PWABuilder (Fastest, 1-Click Online APK Generator)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Upload or host the POS folder on any web hosting (e.g. GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify).
2. Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com in your browser.
3. Enter your POS website URL and click "Start".
4. PWABuilder reads the included "manifest.json" and "sw.js" files automatically.
5. Click "Package for Android" / "Build APK".
6. Download the .apk file and install it directly on any Android tablet!

6. HOW TO USE THE POS (KEY FEATURES)
==============================================================================

1. ADDING AN ITEM TO THE MENU:
   - Click/tap "Add Item" in the top bar.
   - Enter Dish Name (e.g. Ebi Tempura), Price (₱), Category, Description, and photo.
   - Tap "Save Item".

2. EDITING PRICES & MENU ITEMS:
   - Tap "Edit Menu" in the top bar.
   - Tap the pencil icon (Edit) on any dish card to change price or details in-place.
   - Tap "Save Item", then tap "Exit Edit".

3. TAKING AN ORDER:
   - Tap any dish card to add it to the cart on the right.
   - Use "+" and "−" to change quantities (updates instantly with no screen flicker).
   - Tap "Note" to add special instructions (e.g. "Less Spicy", "No Onions", "Extra Sauce").
   - Choose Dine In (with Table #), Take Out, or Delivery.
   - Select discounts if applicable (Senior / PWD 20%, Promo 10%, etc.).

4. PAYMENT & CHANGE:
   - Tap "Charge ₱..." at the bottom of the cart.
   - Choose payment method: Cash, GCash, Card, or QR PH.
   - For Cash: Tap quick bill buttons (+₱50, +₱100, +₱500, +₱1000, or Exact ₱).
   - Change is calculated automatically.
   - Tap "Complete & Print Receipt".

5. THERMAL RECEIPTS:
   - Receipt modal appears in front with item breakdown, discounts, totals, and contact details.
   - Tap "Print Receipt" to output to any standard 58mm or 80mm thermal receipt printer.

7. DATA STORAGE & CSV EXPORT FOR GOOGLE SHEETS
==============================================================================

1. LOCAL PERSISTENCE:
   - All menu catalog items, custom prices, and sales logs are saved automatically 
     inside the device's LocalStorage database.
   - Works 100% offline without needing an active internet connection.

2. EXPORT TO CSV (FOR EXCEL & GOOGLE SHEETS):
   - Tap "Sales" in the top navigation bar.
   - Tap "Export CSV".
   - A .csv file containing all order numbers, dates, times, dining types, payment 
     methods, and sales amounts is downloaded to your device.
   - You can open this file in Microsoft Excel or import it directly into Google Sheets!

8. HOW TO MOVE THIS PROJECT TO ANOTHER COMPUTER / WORK LAPTOP
==============================================================================

To continue coding or running this POS system on another computer:

1. COPY THE ENTIRE FOLDER:
   - Simply copy the "JAI" folder to a USB flash drive or zip and email it.
   - All code, images, and server scripts are 100% self-contained in this single folder.

2. ON THE NEW COMPUTER:
   - Windows: Double-click "run_app.bat".
   - Mac / Linux: Run "bash run_app.sh" or "python3 server.py".
   - Open in any code editor (VS Code, Cursor, etc.) to continue coding.

9. SAFE FEATURE TESTING & REVISION WORKFLOW (test_app.bat & push_to_live.bat)
==============================================================================

To try new features, edit code, or test revisions safely without risking
your live store / cash register:

STEP 1: DEVELOP & TEST IN 'test_app'
  - Double-click "test_app.bat".
  - This launches an isolated test server on Port 8091 (http://localhost:8091).
  - All test files are located inside the "test_app/" folder:
      test_app/index.html
      test_app/app.js
      test_app/style.css
  - Any edits or test orders you do here DO NOT affect the live app.
    Your live database and live server (run_app.bat on Port 8080) stay 100% safe!

STEP 2: PROMOTE TEST APP TO LIVE & GITHUB
  - When your new features are verified and working great:
  - Double-click "push_to_live.bat".
  - It will:
    1. Show you which files were modified.
    2. Automatically save a backup of the current live files into ".backups/".
    3. Copy the updated files from "test_app/" to the live app.
    4. Automatically rebuild the offline bundle (Yamato_POS_Offline.html).
    5. Commit the changes and push directly to GitHub (origin/main).

STEP 3: RESET OR REFRESH TEST APP (OPTIONAL)
  - If you ever want to reset test_app back to match the live app:
  - Double-click "sync_live_to_test.bat".
